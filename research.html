Research

My research is broadly in statistics, probability, and related areas of machine learning. I am particularly interested in high-dimensional inference, random graph models, manifold methods, and random matrix phenomena.

Selected Publications

On the edge eigenvalues of sparse random geometric graphs
Xiucai Ding, Yichen Hu · 2025

This paper studies edge eigenvalues of sparse random geometric graphs generated by multivariate Gaussian samples under broad distance metrics. It develops a smoothing-matching approach to connect graph eigenvalues to a continuum differential operator.

Two sample test for covariance matrices in ultra-high dimension
Xiucai Ding, Yichen Hu, Zhenggang Wang · 2024

We propose an adaptive procedure for testing equality of covariance matrices when the dimension far exceeds the sample sizes. The method combines sample splitting with eigenvalue-based statistics and works under weak structural assumptions.
