Research

My research lies broadly in high-dimensional statistics, graph networks, and their applications in machine learning and data science. I am particularly interested in manifold learning, random geometric graphs, and random matrix theory.

Selected Works

Two Sample Test for Covariance Matrices in Ultra-High Dimension
Xiucai Ding, Yichen Hu, Zhenggang Wang · Journal of the American Statistical Association · 2025

We develop an adaptive procedure for testing the equality of covariance matrices in settings where the dimension greatly exceeds the sample sizes. The method combines sample splitting with eigenvalue-based statistics and remains effective under weak structural assumptions.

On the Edge Eigenvalues of Sparse Random Geometric Graphs
Xiucai Ding, Yichen Hu · 2025

This paper studies the edge eigenvalues of sparse random geometric graphs generated by multivariate Gaussian samples under a broad class of distance metrics. We introduce a smoothing-matching approach that links graph eigenvalues to those of a continuum differential operator.

Adaptive Sparsity-Aware Projection-Assisted Spectral Embedding for High-Dimensional Noisy Data with Nonlinear Structures
Yichen Hu, Xiucai Ding, Jian Kang · In progress

We propose an adaptive projection-assisted spectral embedding method for high-dimensional noisy data with nonlinear structure and latent sparsity. The method denoises pairwise geometry, recovers sparse structure, and enables reliable embedding even in low signal-to-noise settings.
