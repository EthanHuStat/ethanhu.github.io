Ph.D. Candidate in Statistics, University of California, Davis

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Statistics at the University of California, Davis, under the supervision of Xiucai Ding. My research interests include high-dimensional statistics, random matrix theory, manifold learning theory, and graph theory.

Before joining the Ph.D. program, I received my M.S. in Statistics from UC Davis in 2023 and my B.S. in Statistics from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics in 2020.
